## What Changed

- Regional server URLs and the two collection paths (`/v1/messaging/numbers/dedicated`, `/v1/messaging/numbers/sender_address/addresses`) no longer use trailing slashes in the OpenAPI document, code samples, or agent-facing markdown docs.
- Redocly `no-server-trailing-slash` and `no-path-trailing-slash` are elevated from `warn` to `error` so CI blocks regressions.

## Why

Warn-only trailing-slash rules left the Engage OpenAPI contract ambiguous and allowed CI to merge docs that diverge from Redocly recommended URL hygiene.

- `spec`
- `docs/api`
- `redocly.yaml`
