## What Changed

- Public Messages docs and OpenAPI now describe up to 10 RCS `suggestions` (`reply`, `url`, `dial`, `location`) with their required fields, limits, and a request example.
- Docs state that suggestions are echoed on the send response and on message status, that unsupported types such as `calendar` return `400`, and that taps arrive as replies without `postback_data`.
- The legacy `body` shape is documented as not combinable with `suggestions`, and `llms` curation mentions tappable suggestions.

## Why

Tappable RCS suggestions on `/v1/messages` are available to RCS-enabled accounts, and customers need the contract in the developer portal to use them.
