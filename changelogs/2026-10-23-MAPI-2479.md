## What Changed

- Added a Credits section and the Get Credit Balance operation, `GET /v1/credits_management/account`, on the same EU, APAC, and US hosts as the other endpoints.
- Documented the 200 credit-balance body and the 401, 404, and 503 responses. This operation does not return 400, and it does not declare an `Account` header.

## Why

Customers need a published reference for reading an account's remaining MT SMS and MT MMS credits.
