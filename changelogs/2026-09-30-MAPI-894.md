## What Changed

- Create webhook docs now state that an account can have at most 150 webhooks, and `POST /v1/webhooks/messages` returns HTTP 400 with `details` containing `Maximum number of webhooks (150) reached` when the account already has 150 or more.
- The Webhooks Management overview and the curated LLM index describe the same create limit.

## Why

Accounts with very large webhook sets were overflowing inbound notification payloads, so creates are rejected once an account reaches 150 webhooks.
