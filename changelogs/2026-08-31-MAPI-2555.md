## What Changed

- Added the llms.txt v2 `rel="describedby"` discovery link in the docs HTML head while keeping the existing `rel="alternate"` tags for older agents.
- Surfaced a visible **For AI Agents** section on the ReDoc intro and pointed root OpenAPI `externalDocs` at the curated `llms.txt` index.

## Why

AI agents need a standards-correct way to find `llms.txt` from the main docs page and from the OpenAPI document itself, beyond guessing `/llms.txt` at the site root.

## Affected Components

- `web` (site template / discovery tags)
- `spec` (OpenAPI intro and externalDocs)
