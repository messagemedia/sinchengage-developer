## What Changed

- Public Messages docs and OpenAPI now describe `format: "RCS"` with `content`, optional `fallback_message`, and the existing legacy `body[]` shape.
- Send-messages examples and `llms` curation call out plain RCS text and the `402` when the account is not RCS-enabled.

## Why

Plain RCS on `/v1/messages` was available to enabled accounts but missing from the developer portal contract, so customers could not discover the supported payload without internal knowledge.

## Affected Components

- `sinchengage-developer` (OpenAPI + docs)
