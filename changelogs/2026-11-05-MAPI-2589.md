## What Changed

- Public Messages docs and OpenAPI now describe `rich_content.carousel` (1–10 `cards` with the same shape as `rich_content.card`, plus up to 3 carousel `choices`) as an alternative to `content`, `rich_content.media` or `rich_content.card` for `format: "RCS"`, with request and GET-status examples and optional SMS `fallback_message`.
- Docs state that a one-card carousel renders as a single card, that the fallback applies to the whole message, and that the response echoes each card's `suggestions` and the carousel `choices` with `postback_data`.
- Docs list the `400` cases for carousels: combined with `content`, top-level `suggestions`, `media` or `card`; no cards or more than 10; more than 3 choices; an empty card (named by position); card limits inherited from rich cards.
- Shared `rich_content` errors are updated to `rich_content must include media, card or carousel` / `rich_content may contain only one of media, card or carousel` in the media and card sections.
- New `RcsRichCarousel` schema reuses `RcsRichCard` and `RcsSuggestion`.
- `llms` curation mentions RCS carousels.

## Why

RCS carousels on `/v1/messages` (epic MAPI-2367) are available to RCS-enabled accounts, and customers need the contract in the developer portal to use them.
