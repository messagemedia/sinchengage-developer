## What Changed

- The Delivery Reports overview now shows the webhook posted to a message's callback URL for a v1 RCS send (explicit `format: "RCS"` or inferred from `rich_content` / `suggestions`): `content` for text, `content` and `suggestions` for text with suggestions, and `rich_content` for a card, carousel, media file, or location pin. Only a field that was on the message is present.
- `original_text` is omitted when the message is delivered as RCS.
- An RCS message delivered as SMS keeps the SMS delivery report. The original RCS fields may also be included, but customers should not rely on them there. `fallback_message` is not a field on the webhook.
- `GET /v1/delivery_reports` is unchanged and does not return those fields.
- Create webhook documents `$rcsContent`, `$rcsSuggestions`, and `$rcsRichContent` for account webhook templates. The sample template does not include them.

## Why

v1 RCS delivery reports posted to a message's callback URL now echo the RCS request fields. Customers and account webhook templates need that contract in the developer portal.
